5. Withdraw

Paste your saved note. The app finds your deposit on-chain, builds the Merkle path, generates the proof in your browser, checks it against the deployed verifier for free, then submits the withdrawal.

6. Disclose to auditor

Prove to one chosen auditor that you control a specific deposit, without giving them any ability to spend it. The auditor gives you a one-time auditorNonce; the proof is bound to it. What the auditor learns: that deposit's commitment (it is public on-chain anyway) and that you hold its keys. What they do not learn: your keys, your other deposits, or where the funds went when withdrawn.
