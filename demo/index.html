                    Disclose to an auditor

                    Prove to one chosen auditor that you control a specific deposit, without giving them any ability to spend it. They give you a one-time code; the proof only works for that code.

                    The auditor learns
                    • Which deposit (its commitment, already public)
                    • That you hold its keys
                    The auditor does not get
                    • Your spendKey or viewKey
                    • Your other deposits
                    • Where the funds were withdrawn to

                      Report for an accountant or auditor: many notes at once

                      Paste notes and master keys, one per line. One proof is made for every note and all of them go into one file that the auditor checks in one step. Only notes you paste are included.

                      This adds the keys of notes that are already withdrawn, so the auditor can see the recipient, relayer and fee of each withdrawal. Those keys can no longer spend anything, but unlike a proof they are not tied to this auditor: whoever holds them can show them to anyone and can make new disclosures for those notes. Use it for your own accountant, not for strangers. Keys of notes that are not withdrawn are never added.
