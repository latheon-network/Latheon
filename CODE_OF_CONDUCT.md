# Code of conduct

Latheon is open-source privacy infrastructure, built in public. This code applies to everyone who takes part in the project's spaces: the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), the Telegram channel and its discussion group, and any other place where someone speaks for the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and constructive. Criticise designs and code, not people.
- Be honest. Say what you have tested and what you have not; mark guesses as guesses.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- Report security problems privately, as described in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md), never in public issues.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind, including on the basis of sexuality, gender, age, disability, race, religion, ethnicity, nationality, politics or appearance.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, scams, impersonation, and promotion of tokens, airdrops or "investment opportunities". Latheon's LTH is a test token with no value.
- Financial or investment advice given in the project's name.
- Using the project's spaces to plan or encourage illegal activity.

## Reporting

Write to latheon.protocol@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential as far as possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn, mute or ban participants who break this code, in proportion to what happened. Decisions can be questioned by writing to the same address.
