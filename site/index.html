Honest note: the pools hold mostly our own test deposits, so the crowd you hide in is close to empty. The mechanics work and can be checked on-chain; real privacy needs real users.

Honest gas note: gas units differ by network and have changed over time. Our latest single on-chain withdrawals on V5: Ethereum Sepolia 419,919 · Arbitrum Sepolia 276,769 · Robinhood Chain 297,197 (LTH) and 306,346 (USDG). We cannot yet explain the Ethereum figure, so we make no claim that L2 uses more or fewer gas units than L1; fees depend on gas price. A controlled re-measurement is planned.

Older V4 pools stay deployed but are deprecated: their proofs did not bind the recipient. The demo labels them legacy.
