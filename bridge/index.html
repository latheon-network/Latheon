Arbitrum Sepolia → Latheon Testnet
  • Two transactions on Arbitrum Sepolia: an approval for exactly this amount, then the deposit. Both need a little ETH there for gas.
  • The LTH arrives at the same address on the Latheon test chain, usually within a few minutes.
  • Use an ordinary wallet. A smart-contract wallet's deposit lands at a different (aliased) address.
  • Bridging is public: it links your Arbitrum Sepolia address to the same address on the Latheon chain. Privacy starts when you deposit into a pool and withdraw to a new address.
Latheon Testnet → Arbitrum Sepolia
  • A withdrawal has two steps. First, a transaction on the Latheon chain (gas paid in LTH). Then, once the chain's validator has posted the state that includes it and that state is confirmed on Arbitrum Sepolia, a claim there releases the LTH token. Measured so far: about an hour between the two.
  • The claim is a transaction on Arbitrum Sepolia and needs a little ETH for gas. Anyone may send it; the LTH always goes to the address chosen in step one.
  • This page remembers your withdrawals in this browser. On another device, find one by its transaction hash below.
